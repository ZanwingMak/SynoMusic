SynoMusic

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

English

SynoMusic is an unofficial self-hosted music client. It connects to music sources you authorize, including Synology Audio Station, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV, SMB, and local files.

SynoMusic does not sell personal data and does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data stored on your device

  • Server addresses, ports, selected libraries, usernames, and display names.
  • Passwords, tokens, SMB folder bookmarks, and similar credentials are stored in iOS Keychain when you choose to save them.
  • Local playlists, playback history, theme choices, app language, and player preferences.
  • Downloaded songs and imported local music are stored inside the app sandbox.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud configuration sync, SynoMusic may sync server addresses, usernames, local playlists, theme settings, and appearance preferences through your private iCloud account. Passwords and access tokens remain in the local Keychain and are not synced by SynoMusic.

Network services

When you use a connected music source, requests are sent to the server or service you selected. Plex authorization is handled through Plex services. Podcast search, radio browsing, lyrics, artwork, and metadata lookup may contact public third-party services only when you use those features.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at GitHub Issues.

简体中文

SynoMusic 是一个非官方的自托管音乐客户端，可连接你授权的音乐源，包括 Synology Audio Station、Plex、Jellyfin、Emby、WebDAV、SMB 和本地文件。

SynoMusic 不出售个人数据，也不使用第三方广告或跨 App 追踪。

保存在设备上的数据

  • 服务器地址、端口、选定音乐库、用户名和显示名称。
  • 当你选择保存时，密码、Token、SMB 文件夹授权等凭证会保存在 iOS Keychain。
  • 本地歌单、播放历史、主题、App 语言和播放器偏好。
  • 下载歌曲和导入的本地音乐保存在 App 沙盒中。

可选 iCloud 同步

如果启用 iCloud 配置同步，SynoMusic 可能通过你的私人 iCloud 账号同步服务器地址、用户名、本地歌单、主题和外观偏好。密码和访问令牌仍保存在本机 Keychain，不会由 SynoMusic 同步。

网络服务

使用已连接音乐源时，请求会发送到你选择的服务器或服务。Plex 授权由 Plex 服务处理。播客搜索、电台浏览、歌词、封面和元数据查询仅在你使用相关功能时访问公开第三方服务。

联系

如有隐私问题，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系。
