### 描述
SynoMusic 是为私人音乐库打造的原生 iOS 音乐客户端。你可以连接自己授权的 Synology Audio Station、Plex、Jellyfin、Emby、WebDAV、SMB 或本地音乐文件，把分散在不同服务器里的音乐集中到一个精致、快速、适合日常使用的播放器里。

核心功能：
- 多音乐源：Audio Station、Plex、Jellyfin、Emby、WebDAV、SMB、本地导入
- 仅限音乐：不接入视频、电视或照片内容
- 原生播放：锁屏控制、灵动岛、AirPlay、播放队列、睡眠定时
- 歌词与资料：同步歌词、歌词编辑、在线查找、封面和歌曲资料搜索
- 离线能力：歌曲下载、下载队列、本地文件导入、链接下载导入
- 私有配置：多服务器配置、Keychain 凭证、HTTPS、自签名证书信任
- 系统集成：小组件、Siri 捷径、iCloud 配置同步、主题色和备用图标

SynoMusic 是非官方客户端，与 Synology、Plex、Jellyfin、Emby 等项目或公司没有从属或背书关系。你需要自行准备可访问音乐库的账号、Token 或服务器连接信息。

## English

### Subtitle
Private Music Library Player

### Promotional Text
Connect Audio Station, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV, SMB, and local music with native playback, lyrics, downloads, Dynamic Island, and widgets.

### Description
SynoMusic is a native iOS music client for private and self-hosted music libraries. Connect your authorized Synology Audio Station, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV, SMB, or local music files, then enjoy them in a polished player designed for daily listening.

Key features:
- Multiple music sources: Audio Station, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV, SMB, and local imports
- Music only: no video, TV, or photo features are included
- Native playback: Lock Screen controls, Dynamic Island, AirPlay, queue, and sleep timer
- Lyrics and metadata: synced lyrics, lyric editing, online lookup, artwork, and song information search
- Offline support: song downloads, download queue, local file import, and link-based import
- Private configuration: multiple server profiles, Keychain credentials, HTTPS, and self-signed certificate trust
- System integration: widgets, Siri shortcuts, iCloud configuration sync, theme colors, and alternate app icons

SynoMusic is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Synology, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, or related projects. You are responsible for the accounts, tokens, files, and servers you connect.
